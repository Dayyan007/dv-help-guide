Quick questions

Can I use more than one route?

Yes. Many people begin with an NGO or the helpline for advice, then move to a PWPA complaint or a family court case.

Do I need a lawyer?

Not always to start. Protection officers and NGOs can guide you, and legal aid organisations can help you find a lawyer for court.

Will my information be kept private?

Ask each office or organisation directly how they handle confidentiality before you share details.
